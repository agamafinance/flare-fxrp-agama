Why fixed wins when you want certainty

Your payoff is flat. The vault's isn't.

Same deposit, same term. Anchor pays the rate you locked in every scenario; the raw vault pays whatever XRPFi actually yields, which nobody knows in advance.

Anchor (fixed) Raw vault (variable)

The discount closes to par

The AMM's curve flattens to constant-sum as maturity nears, so 1 PT mechanically converges to 1 FXRP. The convergence is math, not a promise.

How it works · under the hood
01

Deposit into a vault

Your FXRP earns real XRPFi yield in an ERC-4626 vault (e.g. Mystic Core FXRP or Bizantine SuperVault), issued as vault shares.

02

Split into PT + YT

The position is tokenized: principal and yield become two separate, tradable tokens.

PT = principal YT = yield
03

Buy PT at a discount

On the YieldSpace AMM you buy PT below par. That discount is your fixed rate, locked at purchase.

04

Redeem 1:1 at maturity

Each PT redeems for exactly 1 FXRP of principal. Your return was fixed on day one, whatever the yield did.
